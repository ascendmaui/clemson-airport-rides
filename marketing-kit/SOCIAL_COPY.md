## Posts

**Ride types**

Four ways to ride: Standard, Wait & Save, Extra Comfort, and Carpool. The quote on the booking screen is the price before you confirm. Book at clemsonrides.com.

**Airports**

Schedule a ride to Greenville-Spartanburg (GSP) or Charlotte Douglas (CLT). The scheduled flats in the product are $75 for GSP and $175 for CLT. Scheduling does not charge the card. Book at clemsonrides.com.

**Student price**

10% off Standard when the signed-in email is confirmed and ends with @clemson.edu or @g.clemson.edu. Other ride types are not included.

**Homepage**

Peace of mind, every ride. Drivers confirm before they come. A backup is ready if they don't. Book at clemsonrides.com.

**Where to book**

Book in the browser at clemsonrides.com. The App Store and Play Store listings are not live. iPhone rider and driver apps have public TestFlight betas.
