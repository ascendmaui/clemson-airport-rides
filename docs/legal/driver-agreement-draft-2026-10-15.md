## 1. Dispatch and Technology Platform Only — Clemson Rides Is Not a Transportation Carrier

1.1 **Platform role.** Clemson Rides ("Clemson Rides," the "Company," "we," or "us") is **only a dispatch service and technology platform** that connects independent drivers with riders who request transportation. Clemson Rides is **not** a transportation carrier, common carrier, contract carrier, motor carrier, taxi company, limousine service, or charter service, and **it does not provide transportation services**. Clemson Rides does not own, lease, operate, or control the vehicles used for trips, and it does not employ the people who drive them.

1.2 **The Driver provides the transportation.** When you accept a ride request, any transportation that follows is provided solely by you, in your own vehicle, on your own time, as an independent business. Clemson Rides is not a party to the act of transporting anyone.

1.3 **No warranties.** To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, Clemson Rides makes no representation or warranty about the volume of requests, the conduct of riders, the accuracy of maps, pickup pins, ETAs, or fare quotes, or the safety of any trip. Section 21 (Disclaimers; AS-IS and AS-AVAILABLE) applies to this Section.

1.4 **Statutory duties.** If the South Carolina Transportation Network Company Act, S.C. Code Ann. §§ 58-23-1610 et seq. (the "TNC Act"), or any other law validly imposes a duty on the Company that a contract cannot disclaim, the Company will perform that duty to the extent the law requires. Performing a statutory duty does not make the Company a carrier, does not make you an employee, and does not expand the Company's liability beyond what the law requires and this Agreement allows.

1.5 **No university affiliation.** Clemson University is not a party to this Agreement, does not operate the platform, and is not responsible for any trip. The word "Clemson" describes the community the platform serves and is not a statement of university sponsorship.

## 10. Ride Types and Extra Comfort Rules

10.1 The Platform offers four Ride Types: **Standard**, **Wait & Save**, **Extra Comfort**, and **Carpool**.

10.2 **Standard.** The base ride. The fare is the quote shown on the booking screen.

10.3 **Wait & Save.** A lower-priced option offered when a driver can serve it, at the fare quoted on that screen.

## 11. Rider Payments: Card Authorization Hold

11.1 **No upfront deposit.** The Company no longer collects a deposit from Riders. When a Rider books a ride, the Rider's card is **authorized as a hold (pre-authorization)** for the estimated fare plus a buffer. For a scheduled ride, the hold is placed when the ride is released to drivers.

11.2 **Capture at or after completion.** The fare is **captured at or after Trip completion**. If the final fare differs from the estimate, the hold is partially captured or increased as the card issuer allows.

11.3 **Release on cancellation.** If a Trip is cancelled under the cancellation policy shown in the app, the hold is **released**, except for any wait or cancellation fee the policy allows.

11.4 **Failed payment.** If a Rider's payment cannot be captured, the Trip may not complete in the system and you may not be paid for it until the balance is collected. The Company does not guarantee payment by any Rider.

11.5 **No cash.** You will not accept a street hail or cash for a ride booked on the Platform.

11.6 **Separate transaction; payment collection agent.** Each Trip is a separate transaction between you and the Rider. The Company collects the fare from the Rider on your behalf as your limited payment collection agent, and the Rider's payment through the Platform satisfies the Rider's obligation to you for that Trip.

## 13. Driver Conduct, Safety, and Trip Codes

13.1 **Pickup (trip) code.** When you reach a Rider, the app shows a short pickup code to the Rider and to you. You will confirm the code before the Rider enters your Vehicle and will not start a Trip with anyone whose code does not match.

13.2 **Status.** You will advance trip status honestly (accepted, arriving, arrived, in progress, completed) and will not mark a Trip complete before drop-off.

13.3 **No subcontracting.** Only you may drive a Trip you accept. You will not let another person use your account.

13.4 **Zero tolerance.** You will not use alcohol, cannabis, or any drug that impairs driving while Online or on a Trip. On a credible report the Company may suspend your access during its inquiry and may deactivate you.

13.5 **Nondiscrimination and service animals.** You will not refuse or end a Trip because of destination, race, color, national origin, religious belief or affiliation, sex, disability, age, or any other characteristic protected by law. You will transport service animals and mobility equipment and will not add a charge because of a disability. You may refuse a Rider who is acting in an unlawful, disorderly, or endangering manner.

13.6 **Respectful conduct.** You will not harass, threaten, touch inappropriately, or make personal comments to a Rider, and will not seek a social contact. Harassment is grounds for immediate deactivation.

13.7 **Safety.** You will not accept a Trip you cannot safely complete, including when fatigued or in unsafe weather. You will obey distracted-driving law and use hands-free features only as the law allows. If anyone is in danger, you will call 911; in-app safety tools are not a substitute for emergency services. You will not carry a weapon where unlawful, transport contraband, or stop where signs, officers, or airport rules forbid.

13.8 **Crashes.** After a crash, injury, or significant loss on a Trip, you will stop when safe, call emergency services if anyone may be hurt, exchange required information, and notify the Company through the app as soon as you safely can. You will not admit fault on the Company's behalf. You will provide the police report number and insurer claim number and will preserve any dash-camera footage and trip records for at least one hundred eighty (180) days, or longer if you know of a claim.

13.9 **Lost items.** You will use the lost-and-found flow rather than keeping a Rider's items.

13.10 **Cancellations and no-shows.** After acceptance you will proceed to the pickup without undue delay. Wait-time, no-show, and cancellation rules shown in the app apply. You will never leave a Rider in an unsafe place as a form of dispute.
